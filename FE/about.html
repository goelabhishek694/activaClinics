ABOUT ACTIVA CLINICS

Ontario’s Most Trusted Multidisciplinary Healthcare Team

Activa Clinics is redefining how healthcare is delivered in Canada. Our unique multidisciplinary model brings a full team of healthcare professionals together under one roof, allowing us to provide seamless, coordinated care that helps patients return to their pre-injury health as safely and efficiently as possible.

With the latest therapeutic technologies, evidence-based treatments, and a highly skilled clinical team, Activa Clinics continues to set the standard for quality care across our communities.

At the heart of everything we do is the patient. Every individual receives a personalized treatment plan designed around their goals, condition, and lifestyle. Whether someone has been injured in a car accident, strained their back at work, slipped while shoveling snow, or developed chronic pain from daily activities, Activa Clinics offers a wide range of services and programs to help them move better, feel better, and live pain-free.

INTEGRATION & INNOVATION

Your Trusted Partner in Modern Healthcare

Since opening one of Ontario’s first multidisciplinary rehabilitation clinics in 1999, Activa Clinics has continued to lead with innovation and patient-focused integration. For over twenty-five years, we have brought together diverse healthcare professionals under one coordinated model to ensure patients receive complete, seamless care.

Today, we continue to evolve with the needs of our communities through modern Tele-Health services, giving patients convenient access to our clinicians—anytime and from anywhere. This commitment to innovation allows us to remove barriers to care and support patients in the comfort of their homes when in-person visits are not possible.

Our focus remains simple and unwavering: empowering patients with effective, personalized care. Whether it’s recovering from an injury, managing chronic pain, or improving mobility and overall well-being, Activa Clinics provides integrated programs and evidence-based treatments to help every patient move forward with confidence.

Pioneer In Healthcare Services

Activa Clinics Is Here To Help

Activa Clinics has long advocated for accessible healthcare for all individuals—no matter where they live, work, or receive care. Our commitment to accessibility has strengthened communities across Ontario, including Aboriginal and First Nations populations, through the introduction of Tele-Health services.

By partnering with local health centres, we help patients in remote areas access medical and paramedical care quickly and reliably, reducing travel barriers and improving continuity of care.

As advisors to several large First Nations communities in Canada, Activa Clinics continues to champion meaningful change by expanding access, supporting local healthcare teams, and promoting equitable wellness for everyone.

In addition to our physical rehabilitation programs, we have also expanded into Mental Health Counselling to further support whole-person wellness. Whether patients are managing post-traumatic stress, postpartum depression, anxiety, or depression, our mental health services are designed to empower them, promote resilience, and help them regain a healthier, more balanced life.

Mission

At Activa Clinics, our mission is to empower and inspire every patient to feel their best and be their best. We are driven by compassion, professionalism, and a genuine commitment to helping individuals regain function, restore confidence, and achieve lasting wellness.

Vision

Since our founding over 20 years ago, our vision has remained unchanged: To keep Patient-Focused Healthcare at the heart of everything we do, and to continue leading the Healthcare, Rehabilitation, and Tele-Health industries through integration, innovation, and patient empowerment—across our communities and across Canada.

Passion

Our passion is helping people move better, feel better, and live better. This passion fuels our purpose and guides every interaction, every treatment plan, and every step of a patient’s healing journey.

Purpose

Our patients are our purpose. Their goals, their health, and their recovery guide every decision we make and every service we provide.
